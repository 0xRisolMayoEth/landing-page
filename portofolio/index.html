The Person Behind The Work

Hi, I'm Galang.

I'm a landing page specialist based in Central Java, Indonesia. I started building websites because I was tired of seeing great local businesses lose customers to slow, generic pages that looked like everyone else's. So I learned to build them properly — by hand, line by line.

I don't touch page builders or recycled templates. Every page I make is written from scratch in clean HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, shaped around your business and the one thing your visitors should do next. That's the difference between a page that just exists and a page that actually sells.
